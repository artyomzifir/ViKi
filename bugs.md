# bugs.md — narrow traps, and what they cost

A running table of the small, specific defects that are expensive to rediscover:
off-by-one constants, settings that silently do nothing, hardware combinations
that do not work. Long investigations live in `docs/`; this file is the index a
tired person reads at midnight.

Add a row when you lose more than an hour to something a single line would have
told you.

## Fixed — kept because the *shape* of the mistake repeats

| # | What was wrong | Where | Fixed by |

| 4 | **The cloud-agreement gate scored partial view overlap, not calibration error, and called a good wide-baseline rig red.** Preset `2-diff` (RealSense + Kinect, 0.60 m apart, optical axes 83° apart): red on NN median 40.6 mm, ICP 200.7 mm / 10.8°, while the bundle it judges reprojects at 0.97 / 1.16 px and an independent `stereoCalibrate` agrees with it to **0.32 mm / 0.02°**. Both gate metrics measure distance to the *nearest point of the other cloud*, so a surface only one camera can see reads as disagreement however good the extrinsics are — here 51 % of the RealSense cloud is outside the Kinect's view, and the one-way NN medians split 42.8 mm vs 8.7 mm. The second mechanism: an empty scene is a plane, and ICP's unconstrained directions on a plane absorbed the bias — of the 217 mm correction, **215.8 mm lay in the plane** and the 12.5° rotation was about an axis 2.4° off its normal. `validate.py`'s own heuristic (large ICP translation + small residual ⇒ mis-registered extrinsics) reads that case exactly backwards. The Kinect+Kinect presets passed at 53° because they overlap enough for the bias to cancel, not because the metric was sound. The 'got worse after re-solving' puzzle (40.6 → 233.7 mm) was the same thing: re-scored against the stored background the re-solved extrinsics give 42.8 mm — the 233.7 mm run scored a different live scene, not a different calibration. | `viki/calibration/validate.py`, `manager.py::_live_camera_cloud` | `pairwise_agreement` now takes a per-device depth frustum and masks each cloud to the other camera's field of view before scoring; `manager._depth_frustum` builds it from the live depth intrinsics, folding the depth→colour rigid transform back out of the colour-camera ChArUco pose. A pair whose masked overlap falls under `CALIB_VALIDATE_MIN_OVERLAP_FRAC` (0.15) is `skipped` with the new verdict **`unknown`** — not scorable is not the same as wrong, so it no longer blocks recording outright, it asks for the same confirm amber does. Measured on the same stored 2-diff background: **red → green**, NN median 42.8 → **7.6 mm**, p90 679 → **14.0 mm**, ICP 217.4 → **15.6 mm**, rotation 12.5° → **1.70°**, shared view 50 % / 66 %. Residual wart: that 7.6 mm now trips the `_SENSOR_NOISE_MM = 8.0` "below sensor noise — likely a metric bug" note, which was tuned against the unmasked metric and misfires on a good masked one. **Second step, after the frustum alone proved insufficient live:** a point can sit inside the other camera's field of view and still be something it never measured — a stereo dropout, a shadow, a surface out of range. Blank a contiguous region of the RealSense depth image and 22.6 % of the Kinect's in-frustum points land on pixels with no reading, carrying an NN median of **41.2 mm** against 6.7 mm for the points the RealSense genuinely saw. So the mask is now frustum *plus* a lookup in the other camera's depth map: it must have a reading there, and that reading must not sit more than 50 mm in front of the point. On the stored plates that takes p90 from 45.7 mm to 12.8 mm under a simulated dropout, and leaves the clean case at 7.5 mm. |

| 21 | **The Validate step could not clear the gate it feeds.** `record_ready` reads the *preset's* `calib/<name>/validation_report.json` and compares its `extrinsics_hash` against the preset's extrinsics; `POST /api/calibration/validate` wrote only the live `data/validation_report.json`. | 26 | **`scene` could only ever prompt an object that happened to stand apart at frame zero, and its clustering grid could not resolve one smaller than ~50 mm.** Run over ten `40mm-cubes-play` takes, the stage failed on all ten before this, for three separate reasons in sequence. | `viki/perception/auto_prompts.py` | Three changes, each forced by a take that failed without it. **(a) The grid follows the object scale.** `min_object_extent_m` (30 mm) tightens the clustering voxel to E/8, because an object of extent E on a grid V is ~(E/V)² voxels per face and has to clear `min_component_points`; at the old 6 mm a 40 mm cube was ~70 voxels and was dropped before ranking. **(b) Frame zero is a preference, not a requirement.** Whether an object is its own component depends on whether a hand is on it at that instant, which the caller cannot know; `frame_attempts` (6) walks frames spread over the take and the manifest records the `seed_frame` that worked. **(c) Colour splits what geometry cannot.** On take 09-58-19 the background plate no longer matched the table, a 382 × 110 × 189 mm slab of tabletop survived as foreground, and the cube was inside it at *every* frame — 0/41 sampled frames had a usable candidate.
