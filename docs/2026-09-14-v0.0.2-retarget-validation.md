# v0.0.2 retarget validation — 2026-09-14

## Scope

This run checks the conservative upper-workspace retarget candidate on four
recorded scenes.  Every input frame was retained: there was no 5 Hz
subsampling.  Candidate plans were written under
`data/_release_validation/v0.0.2/`; the episodes' Viewer plans were not
overwritten.

The reported pose errors are solver tracking/self-consistency errors against
the prepared hand-derived target, not robot or object ground truth.

All four candidate solves converged at native recording rate.  The home
reference plus provisional local-X tool half-turn strongly biases the robot to
the upper workspace, but does not guarantee a top-side solution on every frame:
`pyramid` reaches 81.6%.  Its near-zero floor margin and that of `block-push`
also mean that numerical feasibility is not operational clearance.

## Historical comparison and caveats

`move-shipok` is the closest controlled comparison because candidate and
historical plan share the same 782 observed frames.  Wrist-above-target rises
from 3.6% to 100%; observed position RMSE improves from 9.4 to 7.3 mm, while
observed orientation RMSE worsens from 12.3° to 15.5°.

The other historical plans are useful context, not isolated A/B evidence:

- `pyramid` has 218 observed candidate frames versus 396 historically;
- `cup_grab` has 101 versus 211, and its old adapter length was 50 mm rather
  than the candidate's 11 mm;
- `block-push` has 183 versus 480 observed frames.

Their current absolute orientation errors are nevertheless too large to call
the mapping rotation-aware or pouring-ready.  In particular, `cup_grab` remains
above the target but achieves a downward tool axis on every evaluated frame
while observed orientation RMSE is 68.5°.  This is the expected failure mode of
a branch-biased top-side mapping when task semantics require preserving a
meaningful carried-object rotation.

## Release conclusion

Ship `robot_home` as an explicit, reversible upper-workspace reference policy,
with `zero` retained for comparison.  Treat the current hand-to-tool rotation
as a provisional pick-and-place mapping, not a physical calibration and not a
universal task policy.  Do not enable an unconditional object-relative factor
until segmentation, object identity, pose observability, and contact phases are
measured independently.

Machine-readable measurements are in
`data/_release_validation/v0.0.2/summary.json` (gitignored validation data).
