## MuJoCo gate results

The isolated `viki-experiments` image builds and imports MuJoCo 3.14.0,
Gymnasium 1.2.3 and SB3 2.9.0. The exact cached **old UR10** and ViKi's
Robotiq 2F-85 URDF compile together; MuJoCo imports all five gripper mimic
equalities. On 15 saved arm configurations, MuJoCo versus Pinocchio
`wrist_3_link` FK differs by at most 4.9e-16 m / 7.5e-16 rad. On 15 saved
gripper commands, MuJoCo static pad-center separation differs from ViKi's
`gripper_opening_m` by at most 2.6e-9 m. These are **same-description
self-consistency** checks, not real-hardware accuracy or validated dynamics.

The preliminary table/cube scene uses 40 mm free cubes and episode-derived
red/green XY. Table top z=0 and a 0.7 × 1.2 m footprint are provisional
frame/layout assumptions. The first dynamic replay is **not evaluable**:
with current uncalibrated actuator/constraint dynamics, gripper and/or arm
tracking diverges during approach. `replay.py` now stops and reports
`valid_tracking=false`, `released_stack_success=null`; it must not be counted
as a task failure or as training data. No BC/SAC/SmolVLA policy has been
trained, and no simulated stack success has been established.

Next: validate gripper actuator inertia/servo and internal collision pairs,
then replay the saved `q_approach` plus demonstration with bounded tracking
error and realistic pad contacts. Only after one stable physical replay should
we collect success-filtered trajectories and run the BC/SAC/RLPD comparison
defined in `PLAN.md`.
