\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:concl}

\section{Summary}
\label{sec:summary}

ViKi implements an offline route from multi-view RGB-D capture to
separate hand, object and candidate robot-plan artifacts. This thesis
reports the retained, reproducible HO-Cap perception evaluation, not
an end-to-end robot-learning result. The earlier local cube recordings
and their downstream artifacts are unavailable, so their numerical
summaries and derived simulation or policy claims are excluded.

\section{Answers to the Research Questions}
\label{sec:answers}

\textbf{RQ1: pipeline yield.} The external eight-view HO-Cap evaluation
completed on 28 selected sequences and 29,706 frames. Its prepared hand
tracks have 10.79~mm matched-joint-weighted MPJPE against the published
semi-automatic reference, with 623,826/623,826 valid joints matched.
For 112 named object tracks, macro-mean relative translation and rotation
drift are 5.44~mm and 7.58 degrees after first-frame alignment and with
published instance masks. Large orientation failures remain, including
112.60 degrees mean error on one projector-remote track. These data
quantify external perception, not the local Kinect-to-robot pipeline
yield or absolute object pose accuracy.

\textbf{RQ2: object-aware transfer.} No retained local demonstration
and matched simulator evaluation supports a claim about physics-validated
transfer to new cube placements. The question remains open.

\textbf{RQ3: learning benefit.} No reproducible comparison against
a matched no-ViKi learner is available. The benefit of ViKi-derived
examples or their sample efficiency is not established.

\textbf{RQ4: visual policy.} No evaluated image-conditioned robot policy
is reported.

\section{Claim Boundary and Next Validation}
\label{sec:contrib-final}

The current evidence supports a measurable HO-Cap hand-reconstruction
result and a conditional, relative object-tracking result. It does
not validate local camera calibration, complete cube-transfer
demonstrations, collision-free grasping, physical simulator success,
policy generalization or real-robot transfer. Re-recording a
preserved local cohort with explicit calibration controls is necessary
before testing those claims. Each subsequent retarget, physics and
learning result must retain its input scenes, protocol and numerical
outputs so it can be independently checked.

\section{Concluding Remarks}
\label{sec:remarks}

The distinction between a reconstructed hand, a tracked object, a
geometric robot plan and a successful manipulation is essential.
Only the first two have quantitative external evidence in this
manuscript; the latter stages remain work to evaluate.
